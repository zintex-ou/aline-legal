What Aline does

You pick a front-facing photo from your library. Aline finds the points of your face and lets you adjust the shape of the nose, lips, chin, jaw and cheekbones with sliders, so you can see what a change would look like before you decide anything.

Aline is a visual simulation, not a prediction. It does not forecast the outcome of any procedure, it is not a medical device, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Only a qualified clinician can tell you what is possible for your face.

Common questions

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Photos are read from your library, processed on the device and never uploaded by us. See the privacy policy for the one exception — an optional feature that you have to confirm each time.

The app says it cannot find a face

Use a photo taken straight on or in profile, in even light, with the whole face visible. Sunglasses, heavy shadows and extreme angles will defeat the detector.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge.

How do I delete my data?

Aline has no accounts and stores nothing about you on our side. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the device.
