Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Aline is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia ("we"). This policy explains what the Aline iPhone app does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Your photos stay on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers holding your images, and no advertising or tracking SDKs. We cannot see your face, and we do not want to.

Photos

When you choose a photo, iOS gives the app access to that image. The face points are detected on your device and every adjustment you make is a calculation performed on your device. The photo is held in memory while you work and is not written to any server of ours, not logged, and not shared with anyone.

Aline asks for photo library permission only so you can pick an image. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or health access, and it does not read your library in the background.

The one time something leaves the device

Aline will offer an optional step that sends the image you are working on to a third-party AI provider to render a more photorealistic version. This step never runs by itself:

  • it happens only when you tap the button for it;
  • a screen tells you which provider will receive the image before it is sent;
  • the provider processes the image to return a result and, under our agreement with them, does not use it to train their models;
  • if you never use that feature, no image ever leaves your phone.

Images produced by that step carry a visible marker showing they are simulated, as required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email or password — the app has no sign-in.
  • No name, address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or attribution SDKs.
  • No health data. Aline is not connected to Apple Health and records nothing about your body or any procedure.
  • No face templates. The points detected on your photo are used to draw the preview and are discarded when you close the photo. They are never used to identify or recognise you, and never stored by us.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription, Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat as our subscription infrastructure; it receives a random identifier generated for your installation, the product you bought and whether the subscription is active, so the app knows what to unlock. That identifier is not linked to your name or your photos.

Diagnostics

If you have Apple's "Share with App Developers" setting switched on, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These contain technical information about the crash, never your photos. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Aline is not directed at children and is rated 13+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Everything the app keeps lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. If you believe we hold something about you, write to info@zintex.dev and we will look into it. Under the GDPR you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
